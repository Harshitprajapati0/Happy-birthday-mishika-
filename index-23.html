14 / Open when you're ready

The letter I couldn't fit into a chat

Dear little sister,

Today I wanted to give you something that doesn't need a courier, a hostel gate, a bus ticket, or a perfect plan. So I made you a tiny world on the internet.

People sometimes think “sister” is a word decided by a family tree. For me, you became my sister through the things that can't be measured: the conversations, the trust, the stupid jokes, the caring, the little arguments, the moments when we simply knew the other person would understand.

Blood made us strangers… but the heart made us siblings.

And if someone ever asks me whether you're my real sister, I won't need a complicated answer. Real sister by blood? No. Real sister by bond? Absolutely.

On your birthday, I don't wish you a perfect life. I wish you a life with enough courage to handle the difficult days, enough laughter to survive the silly ones, enough kindness around you, and enough confidence to know that you deserve good things.

Distance can change where we are sitting. It cannot decide how much someone matters.

So wherever you are on 6 October, please remember this: somewhere in a hostel, your annoying brother is smiling because you exist.

Happy Birthday, little sister. Keep your heart soft, your dreams big, and your laughter loud.

— Your brother, always. 💗
